## 🌱 About the Project

NFTNAFAKA is a project created with the intention of sharing knowledge about blockchain technology and the Web3 world in a **simple, friendly, and approachable way**.

The goal of the project is education — without pressure, hype, or unrealistic promises.  
NFTNAFAKA exists to help people feel more confident, informed, and comfortable navigating the digital space.

---

## 🎓 Education & Utility

By purchasing **NFTNAFAKA**, users receive:

- ✔ a digital NFT access pass to online education  
- ✔ access to educational content  
- ✔ access to the community  
- ✔ additional digital benefits within the project  

The education covers:
- Blockchain fundamentals  
- Web3 concepts  
- Tokens (utility-focused)  
- NFT technology  
- Meme coin phenomenon  

---

## 🪙 SFT Tokens (Additional Benefits)

By purchasing NFTNAFAKA, users automatically receive **SFT tokens**.

SFT tokens are used exclusively for:
- additional educational benefits  
- discounts on future educational programs  
- access to specific content or community features  

SFT tokens:
- ❌ have no investment purpose  
- ❌ do not represent a financial instrument  
- ❌ are not intended for trading or profit  

Their purpose is **purely functional and educational**.

---

## ⚠️ Important Notice

NFTNAFAKA:
- is not an investment  
- does not constitute financial advice  
- does not promise profit or financial returns  

The price of the NFT represents the **value of education, project development, and digital benefits**, not an investment.

---

## 🌐 How It Works

1. Purchase NFTNAFAKA  
2. Connect your wallet to the web application  
3. Ownership verification  
4. Access the education and content  

NFT and SFT tokens remain in the user’s wallet.
